黑熊生成提示詞（供未來重製參考）
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a production 4-frame sprite sheet for a website loading animation, not a mockup. Create ONE square PNG, transparent background, a precise 2 by 2 equal grid of four animation frames with NO grid lines. Same adorable original Taiwanese black bear in each frame, clearly recognizable white V-shaped chest patch, rounded charcoal black body, small rounded ears, warm caramel muzzle, rosy cheeks, little oval eyes; excellent polished contemporary mascot illustration with crisp clean outlines and subtly soft highlights, charming enough for an event kiosk. Each frame: one full body bear holding one transparent cup of milky beige bubble tea with clearly visible dark tapioca pearls and one muted green straw. No clothes, no letters, no logos. Bear must be large and centered within each quarter, identical character design, camera, scale and foot baseline in all frames, plenty of transparent space between quadrants so each can be used separately. Top-left frame: relaxed happy bear holding cup in its LEFT paw (viewer right), other paw resting down. Top-right frame: bear raises cup and actually sips through straw, content closed eyes, tiny blush. Bottom-left frame: delighted eyes, holding cup in same left paw at chest level, leaning a little playfully left as if swaying to music, same basic foot position. Bottom-right frame: cup in same left paw, right paw raised in friendly wave, smiling. All four distinct poses must appear exactly once. Entire illustration stays within its own quarter with 10% padding each side. Transparent outside each bear, no scenery, no shadows extending to neighboring frames, no text anywhere. High definition, fine smooth edges. Square canvas ideally 2048 by 2048.
